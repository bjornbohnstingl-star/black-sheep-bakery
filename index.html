About

the story behind the sheep

Black Sheep Bakery is a small, home-based cake pop business run by Christie Bohnstingl. Every cake pop is baked, dipped, and decorated by hand in small batches — made for birthdays, showers, holidays, and anyone who wants dessert that's a little different from the herd.
