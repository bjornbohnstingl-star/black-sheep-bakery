About

the story behind the sheep

Black Sheep Bakery is a small, home-based dessert studio run by Christie Bohnstingl. Cake pops, custom cakes, cookies, and whatever sweet thing you're picturing — everything is baked and decorated by hand in small batches, made for birthdays, showers, holidays, and anyone who wants dessert that's a little different from the flock.
